# Sprint 2 triage — why did the six bad answers go wrong?

`docs/Sprints.md` requires triaging Sprint 1's failures before fixing anything:
separate **bad retrieval** from **missing/contradictory KB content** from **bad
chunking** from **generation** (the model ignoring the context it was given).
Input: the six problematic answers from
`docs/eval/Hallucination-Grading-2026-09-19.md` (3 hallucinated, 3 grounded but
unresponsive). Issue: #5.

## Method

For each question, ran the tier's retriever exactly as production does
(`similarity_search_with_score`, top-4 chunks of the persisted Chroma index) and
checked whether the sentence that decides the answer was inside the retrieved
chunks. Chunking config: `RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter`, 2000 chars, 500
overlap (`tools/rag_responder.py`).

Index shape: free 14 chunks (compliance 9, payments 3, locations 1, pos 1),
paid 15 chunks (compliance 9, payments 3, locations 1, pos 2).

## Result

| Entry | Decisive sentence | In top-4? | Rank of its chunk | Cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `rag-adv-001` (free, multiple locations?) | "single location" (`free/locations.txt`) | yes | #1 | **Generation** |
| `rag-adv-002` (free, channels + locations?) | "single location" | yes | #1 | **Generation** |
| `rag-free-004` (free, how many locations?) | "single location" | yes | #1 | **Generation** (answered with the tier-neutral "depends on your plan") |
| `rag-paid-001` (paid, how many locations?) | "set up multiple locations" (`paid/locations.txt`) | yes | #1 | **Generation** (same evasive answer) |
| `rag-adv-004` (free, POS hardware?) | "Free Subscription doesn't allow to sell in person" (`free/pos.txt:21`) | yes | #1 | **KB contradiction + generation**: same chunk also says you can sell in person (lines 3, 7); see #6 |
| `rag-oos-002` (support hours / phone) | none exists | n/a | n/a | **Generation**: retrieval returned unrelated payments/POS chunks (expected, the KB has nothing on this) and the model answered from general knowledge instead of saying it isn't covered |

## Conclusions

1. **Retrieval and chunking are not the cause of any of the six.** This matches
   retrieval recall@k = 100% and tier leakage = 0%. Nothing in the retriever or
   chunker needs fixing to address #5.
2. **The hallucination problem is in the generation step**: the model is given
   the deciding sentence (top-ranked chunk) and answers against it or around it.
3. **One KB content defect is confirmed**: `free/pos.txt` contradicts itself
   (#6). It affects `rag-adv-004` only.
4. **Context is noisy, which plausibly makes it worse (hypothesis, not tested).**
   Each answer is built from four chunks of roughly 1,200-1,800 characters, and
   for the location questions two or three of the four are unrelated
   compliance/POS/payments text. `compliance.txt` is 9 of the 14 free chunks and
   repeats its opening paragraphs, so it is frequent filler. A 3B model may be
   distracted by it.

## Implications for Sprint 2

The sprint is titled "Knowledge base & retrieval hardening", but the evidence
says the highest-value fix is in generation, not retrieval. Candidate levers,
to be tested one at a time and re-scored on the same 15 `rag-*` entries plus a
full 38-entry regression run:

- A stricter answer prompt (answer only from context; quote the sentence that
  answers; say "not covered" otherwise).
- Fewer or smaller retrieved chunks to reduce noise (`k`, chunk size), checked
  against recall@k so it doesn't regress.
- Rewording `locations.txt` so the tier limit isn't next to tier-neutral
  sentences (KB content).
- A larger model (last resort; changes the project's local-only story).

The reindex script from the original backlog is still needed: any KB edit
currently requires manually deleting `chroma_db/`, because the index is reused
when populated (`tools/rag_responder.py:34`).

## Caveats

Single retrieval query per question, no repeated runs, and the retrieval check
shows the decisive sentence *was present*, not that the model *read* it. That
the model ignored it is inferred from the answers, not observed inside the model.
