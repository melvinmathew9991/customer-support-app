Point of sale

Brightstall POS lets you sell your products in person in shops, at pop-ups and at other locations.
Whether you are opening your first Brightstall store or adding in-person sales to an online store, the Getting Started guide walks through setting up the Brightstall POS app, including picking the subscription that suits you, and the Hardware overview describes the devices you can use for in-person selling.

Brightstall POS
Brightstall POS is a point of sale app for selling in person. You can use it almost anywhere, including permanent shops, markets and pop-up stalls. The Brightstall POS app runs only on iOS and Android devices.

Brightstall POS keeps your orders and inventory in sync across your shops, your online store and your other active sales channels. You can also view and manage your store's orders from inside the app.

Hardware
Brightstall works with a range of retail hardware for selling in person and online. If your store is based in a supported country, you can buy compatible hardware directly from Brightstall.

The Hardware section explains which devices suit which kinds of store.

Managing Brightstall POS
Once your store is set up and your hardware has arrived, you can start selling with Brightstall POS.

The Selling in person section covers how to manage your store's in-person sales.

Selling in person
The core features of the Brightstall POS app work almost anywhere, including permanent shops, markets and pop-up stalls. You build a cart for each customer and can change it before checkout, for example by adding or removing products or applying a discount. When the cart is ready, the customer can pay with any of the payment methods you accept.
Brightstall POS syncs with your Brightstall admin so that orders and inventory stay up to date across your shops, your online store and your other active sales channels. You can view and manage your store's orders from the Brightstall POS app.
