Payments
At checkout, your customers can pay with any payment method you have turned on in the Payment providers area of your Brightstall admin. Brightstall supports a range of payment methods.
When you decide which methods to offer, start with cards: to accept credit card payments you can use Brightstall Payments or a third-party payment provider.
Customers can also pay online without a credit card, for example with a digital wallet or another alternative payment service. Some stores also choose to accept other methods, such as cryptocurrency.

How you get paid
Every credit card payment has to be processed, so there is usually a gap between the moment a customer pays and the moment you receive the money. Once processing is complete, the amount is transferred to your merchant account.
Other payment providers move money from your customers to you in their own ways. Ask the provider you use how and when it pays you. Unless you use Brightstall Payments as your payment provider, you pay a third-party transaction fee on every order that Brightstall does not process financially itself, including orders that go through the Brightstall checkout.
With Brightstall Payments turned on, you receive your money as Brightstall payouts whenever a customer pays through Brightstall Payments or a supported express checkout. Payments taken through a third-party payment gateway do not show up as payout information in your Brightstall admin.

How often you get paid
With Brightstall Payments, you can check your pay period to see when your payouts for credit card orders will arrive. Other payment providers set their own payout schedules for credit card orders; ask your provider how often you will be paid.
A payout is not always in your bank account the moment it is sent. It can take a few days after a payout is sent for the money to be deposited. If your payouts seem delayed, check with your bank.
If you change banks, or your bank account details change, you can update them in your Brightstall Payments settings.

Pay in Parts
When you capture the payment for an order paid with Pay in Parts, you receive the full order amount within 1 to 3 business days, minus the Pay in Parts fee. You never have to collect the instalments from your customer yourself.
Pay in Parts payouts come from our lending partner, Crestline Credit, and go to the same bank account you use for Brightstall Payments. Custom payout schedules do not apply to Pay in Parts payouts.

How much you get paid
Online transactions can carry several third-party transaction fees. For a credit card transaction, the issuer, the acquirer and the card network each take a small fee for their part.
Orders processed through Brightstall Payments carry no third-party transaction fees. You still pay credit card processing fees, which depend on your Brightstall subscription plan. If you use a third-party payment provider with Brightstall, you pay a third-party transaction fee.

How a credit card payment is processed
When a customer pays for an online order by credit card, the payment is processed before the money reaches your merchant account. Your payment provider handles the processing for you, in four stages:

Authorization
Capture
Clearing
Funding

Authorization
The customer pays for the order in your store with their credit card. Your payment provider asks the card issuer whether the card is valid. If it is valid and has enough available funds, the issuer authorizes the payment. No money moves at this stage.

Capture
Once a payment is authorized, it has to be captured. Capturing a payment sends its details to the acquirer.

Clearing
The acquirer checks the payment details and requests the funds from the card network that processes the customer's card.

The card network passes the transaction to the issuer. The issuer keeps a small fee and sends the rest to the card network, which keeps its own fee and sends the remainder to the acquirer.

Funding
The acquirer keeps a small fee and transfers the final amount to your merchant account.

Manual payment methods
Manual payment methods are not allowed on a free subscription. On a free subscription your customers must pay through your online checkout: you cannot accept payments outside it, such as cash on delivery, money orders, bank transfers or email money transfers. Manual payment methods require a paid subscription.
