Locations
In Brightstall, a location is any place, physical or digital, where your business does at least one of these things:

keeps inventory in stock
sells products
packs, ships or fulfills orders

Paid subscription: your Brightstall store can have multiple locations, so you are not limited to one. Brightstall uses your locations to track inventory and fulfill orders.

A location can be a shop, a warehouse, a market stall, a pop-up, a dropshipping partner, or anywhere else you keep or manage stock.

Running several locations gives you a clearer picture of your inventory across the whole business.

Online orders and in-person sales are each assigned to a location so your inventory counts stay correct.
When an item is out of stock at one location but available at another, Brightstall can split the order so that each part ships from a location that has the stock.

If you sell in person with the Brightstall POS app, you can add a location to each sales device so that its sales are counted against the right inventory.

A location that you deactivate does not count toward your location limit.

An app that stocks inventory or fulfills orders on your behalf is also treated as a location, but it does not count toward the location limit. Brightstall adds the app as a location automatically when you install it.

A custom app built only for your store may need an update before it can track inventory across multiple locations. Its developer can find the migration notes in the Brightstall developer documentation.
