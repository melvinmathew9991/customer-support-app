Locations
In Brightstall, a location is any place, physical or digital, where your business does at least one of these things:

keeps inventory in stock
sells products
packs, ships or fulfills orders

Free subscription: your Brightstall store can have only one location. Adding a second location is not possible on a free subscription. Multiple locations require a paid subscription.

Your one location is where Brightstall tracks your inventory and fulfills your orders. It can be your online store itself or the place where you keep your stock.

Every online order is assigned to that location so your inventory counts stay correct.

An app that stocks inventory or fulfills orders on your behalf is also treated as a location, but it does not count toward the location limit. Brightstall adds the app as a location automatically when you install it.
