Locations
A location is any physical place or an app where you do any or all of the following activities:

sell products
ship or fulfill orders
stock inventory

Free subscription: you can set up only a single location in your Shopify store. You cannot set up multiple locations on a free subscription. Multiple locations are available only with a paid subscription.

You use your single location to track inventory and fulfill orders. Your location can be your online store or the place where you stock inventory.

To make sure your inventory quantities are accurate, online orders are assigned to your location.

Apps that stock inventory or fulfill orders for you are considered to be locations, but don't count towards the location limit. When you add such an app to your store, the app is automatically added as a location.
